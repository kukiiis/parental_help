Cross-Border Family Context (Czech Citizen Father + Foreign-National Mother): A child born to at least one Czech citizen parent generally acquires Czech citizenship automatically by descent regardless of place of birth. Confirm Georgian citizenship and dual-citizenship formalities separately with the Georgian Embassy, and never conflate citizenship procedures with residence permits.

🚨 Urgent Red-Flag Rule (< 3 Months Old): A baby younger than 3 months with a temperature of 38.0 °C or higher requires prompt medical assessment. Call 155 (or 112) immediately for laboured/fast breathing, pauses in breathing, blue/grey lips or skin, unresponsiveness, or seizures. Never give medicines or doses based on an online guide—use clinician-provided instructions for your child's exact age and weight.

🛡️ Safe Sleep, Car Seat, Weather & Feeding Essentials i
Safety First
  • Safe Sleep (Every Sleep): Place baby on the back on a firm, flat, separate sleep surface with a fitted sheet. Keep pillows, loose blankets, bumpers, nests, positioners, and soft toys out of the sleep space. Avoid overheating and weighted sleep products.
  • Car Seat & Cold Weather: Use a correctly fitted rear-facing infant seat. Never put bulky coats or snowsuits under the harness—secure the harness snugly over thin warm layers, then add a blanket over the fastened harness.
  • Outdoor & Indoor Climate: Dress in breathable layers; check baby's temperature at the chest or back of the neck rather than hands/feet, and remove warm layers promptly indoors to avoid overheating.
  • Feeding & Solids: Discuss feeding cues, wet nappies, and weight gain with PLDD. When introducing solids, ask about iron-rich foods and safe allergens. Strictly avoid honey before 12 months and whole nuts/hard choking hazards.
ℹ️ Plain-Language Guide to Czech Family Benefits (PPM, Otcovská & RP)
  • PPM (Peněžitá pomoc v mateřství — Maternity Benefit): Paid by ČSSZ for 28 weeks (196 days) for one child, or 37 weeks (259 days) for multiples, usually starting 6–8 weeks before the due date at 70% of the reduced daily assessment base (RDVZ).
  • Otcovská (Paternity Benefit): 14 consecutive calendar days for the father, started within 6 weeks after birth (separate from the mother's PPM) at 70% of RDVZ.
  • Rodičovský příspěvek (RP — Parental Allowance): Flexible total pool of 400,000 Kč for a single youngest child born/adopted from 1 January 2027 (Act No. 152/2026 Sb.; 350,000 Kč for births prior to 2027). Monthly drawdown speed can be adjusted every 3 months up to the higher eligible parent's monthly cap.

🧾 Tax & OSVČ vedlejší Decision Rules
Verify Current Tax Year Rules
  • Work During PPM / Otcovská: Receiving PPM or Otcovská restricts performing the specific insured work from which the benefit is claimed (e.g., main employment contract). Separate contracts or secondary OSVČ vedlejší activities are assessed separately—confirm with payroll/ČSSZ.
  • Child Tax Benefit (Daňové zvýhodnění): Decide which parent claims the child each month; both parents cannot claim the same child in the same month. Keep proof of shared household and non-claim by the other parent.
  • Spouse Tax Credit (Sleva na manžela/manželku): Conditional on sharing a household with a dependent child under age 3 and staying below the spouse's annual own-income ceiling. Note that PPM counts toward the spouse's own-income limit, while Rodičovský příspěvek is excluded—verify exact statutory rules for the relevant tax year.
  • OSVČ vedlejší Transitions: Report changes (such as PPM ending and RP or care of a child under age 4 beginning) promptly to ČSSZ and your health insurer so secondary status and advance exemptions stay properly registered.
ℹ️ How to Use the Child Development & Play Roadmap (Ages 0–6)
This is a flexible, evidence-informed guide for a typically developing child—not a diagnostic test or therapy protocol. Children develop at different rates and use different movement strategies (crawling is one option, alongside rolling, pivoting, or bottom-shuffling). Follow your child's cues, avoid milestone-forcing equipment (like wheeled baby walkers), and consult a paediatrician promptly if a child loses a previously acquired skill or shows persistent asymmetry or feeding difficulties.
🌱 Five Developmental Domains to Nurture Together
Whole-Child Approach
1. Gross Motor
Floor play, reaching, rolling, self-chosen mobility, climbing, running, jumping, balance, and varied outdoor movement.
2. Fine Motor
Grasping, transferring between hands, stacking, scribbling, turning pages, posting large objects, safe utensils, and dressing practice.
3. Language & Communication
Talk during routines, respond to sounds/gestures, sing, read in family languages, name objects, and pause for turn-taking responses.
4. Cognitive / Problem-Solving
Cause-and-effect toys, object permanence games, sorting, building, pretend play, puzzles, and open-ended materials.
5. Social-Emotional
Warm responsive care, imitation, shared attention, naming feelings, turn-taking, predictable routines, and peer play.
WHO Screen & Restraint Rules
Max 1 hour restrained at a time (car seat/stroller/carrier). Zero sedentary screen time under age 2 (except caregiver video-chats).
