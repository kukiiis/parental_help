Cross-Border Family Profile (Czech Father + Georgian Mother with Permanent Residence): A child born to a Czech citizen father generally acquires Czech citizenship automatically by descent regardless of birth location, and may also qualify for Georgian citizenship through the mother (confirm dual-citizenship formalities with the Georgian Embassy before birth). Do not conflate citizenship with residence procedures.
🚨 Urgent Red-Flag Rule (< 3 Months): A baby younger than 3 months with a temperature of 38.0 °C or higher needs prompt medical assessment. Call 155 (or 112) immediately for breathing difficulty, pauses in breathing, blue/grey lips or skin, unresponsiveness, or seizures. Never give medicines or doses based on an online guide—use clinician-provided instructions for your baby's exact age and weight.

🍼 Feeding & Safe Sleep / Winter Rules
Evidence Safeguards
  • Feeding & Solids: Discuss breastfeeding, expressed milk, formula, or combo feeding with PLDD. When starting solids, ask about iron-rich foods and safe allergen introduction. Strictly avoid honey before 12 months and whole nuts/hard choking hazards.
  • Safe Sleep (Every Sleep): Place baby on the back on a firm, flat, separate sleep surface with a fitted sheet. Keep pillows, loose blankets, bumpers, nests, positioners, and soft toys out of the sleep space. Avoid overheating and weighted sleep products.
  • Winter Car-Seat Rule: Use a rear-facing infant seat compatible with your vehicle. Never put bulky coats or snowsuits under the harness—buckle the harness snugly over thin warm layers, then place a blanket or approved cover over the fastened harness.
  • Temperature Check: Check baby's chest or back of the neck rather than hands/feet, which are normally cooler in winter.
2027 Legislative Update (Act No. 152/2026 Sb.): For a single youngest child born or taken into permanent care from 1 January 2027, the statutory Rodičovský příspěvek (RP) pool is 400,000 Kč (increased from 350,000 Kč). 2027 sickness-insurance reduction thresholds (MPSV No. 185/2026 Sb.) are 1,723 Kč / 2,584 Kč / 5,167 Kč per day.

🧾 Tax, Otcovská & OSVČ vedlejší Decision Rules
Verify 2027 Tax Instructions
  • Father's Work During Otcovská / PPM: Taking PPM or Otcovská restricts performing the specific insured activity from which the benefit is drawn (e.g., main employment contract). Separate activities (such as OSVČ vedlejší or another contract) are assessed separately—confirm with payroll/ČSSZ.
  • Child Tax Benefit (Daňové zvýhodnění): Decide which parent claims the child each month; both parents cannot claim the same child in the same month. Keep proof of shared household and non-claim by the other parent.
  • Spouse Tax Credit (Sleva na manžela/manželku): Conditional on sharing a household with a child under age 3 and meeting the statutory own-income limit. Note that PPM counts toward the spouse's own-income limit, whereas Rodičovský příspěvek is excluded—verify exact 2027 statutory definitions before filing.
  • Employment + OSVČ vedlejší: Report transitions (such as PPM ending and RP/childcare under age 4 starting) to ČSSZ and your health insurer promptly so secondary status and advance exemptions remain properly documented. Compare actual expenses vs. flat-rate percentages across income tax, social, and health bases combined.
Flexible Evidence-Informed Guide (Birth to Age 6): Milestone ranges are ideas, not pass/fail tests or deadlines. Follow your child's cues, avoid equipment designed to force milestones (like wheeled baby walkers), and contact your paediatrician promptly if a child loses a previously acquired skill or shows persistent asymmetry, stiffness/floppiness, or feeding difficulties.
🌱 Five Developmental Domains to Nurture Together
Whole-Child Framework
1. Gross Motor
Floor play, reaching, rolling, self-chosen mobility (crawling, rolling, or shuffling), climbing, running, jumping, balance, and outdoor movement.
2. Fine Motor
Grasping, transferring between hands, stacking, scribbling, turning pages, posting large objects, safe utensils, crafts, and dressing practice.
3. Language & Communication
Talk during routines, respond to sounds/gestures, sing, read in family languages, name objects, and pause to allow turn-taking responses.
4. Cognitive / Problem-Solving
Cause-and-effect toys, object permanence games, sorting, building, pretend play, puzzles, and open-ended household exploration.
5. Social-Emotional
Warm responsive care, imitation, shared attention, naming feelings, turn-taking, predictable routines, and peer play opportunities.
WHO Screen & Restraint Rules
Max 1 hour restrained at a time (car seat/stroller/carrier). Zero sedentary screen time under age 2 (except caregiver video-chats).
