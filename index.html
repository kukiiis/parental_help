🛏️ AAP Safe Sleep Essentials (Every Sleep)

  • ✓ Back to sleep: Always place baby on their back on a firm, flat, non-inclined mattress with a fitted sheet.
  • ✓ Bare sleep space: No pillows, crib bumpers, baby nests/pods, positioners, loose blankets, or soft toys.
  • ✓ Avoid overheating: Use a properly sized, non-weighted sleep sack. No single TOG or humidity number fits every home—check baby's cues.
  • ✓ Room-sharing ≠ bed-sharing: Room-sharing in a separate crib/bassinet is recommended. Note: a stroller bassinet is not automatically an overnight safe-sleep space.

🚗 Winter Car-Seat Safety Rule (AAP)

  • ✕ No bulky coats or snowsuits under the harness: Puffy padding compresses in a crash, leaving dangerous slack.
  • ✓ Layer properly: Dress baby in warm thin layers + hat, buckle the harness snugly first, then tuck a blanket over the secured harness.
  • ✓ Approved accessories only: Use newborn inserts or covers only if explicitly approved by the car-seat manufacturer.

Spouse Tax Credit (Sleva na manžela/ku)

2026 Reference: 24,840 Kč/year (prorated monthly for months where conditions are met on the 1st day) if sharing a household with a dependent child under age 3 and spouse's own annual income is ≤ 68,000 Kč.

Crucial Distinction: PPM counts toward the 68,000 Kč spouse income cap! Rodičovský příspěvek (RP) is excluded. Include all other income categories, not just OSVČ turnover.

OSVČ Vedlejší During Childcare

Caring for a child under age 4 qualifies as a secondary self-employment (vedlejší) reason for social insurance (only one parent per child/month; submit ČSSZ declaration if RP isn't paid or after it ends).

VZP Health Insurance: Even if minimum monthly advances do not apply, actual annual profit can still create a year-end contribution settlement.

Evidence-Based Movement, Footwear & Sports Guidance

🪑 Sitting & Standing

Do not force prolonged unsupported sitting or pull a child upright to force walking. Brief supported play is fine when head/trunk control allow.

🚫 Walkers & Jumpers

Wheeled baby walkers are discouraged due to injury risk and do not teach independent walking. Jumpers must never replace free floor play.

🧦 First Shoes & Barefoot

Barefoot or non-slip socks indoors are ideal on safe surfaces. First shoes should fit well and allow toe movement; 'barefoot' branding is not a medical requirement.

🚲 Bikes & Swimming

Introduce a balance bike when interested and able to straddle safely (~18–24m is a possible window, not a target). Swimming lessons never make a child drown-proof—keep touch supervision.

👶 Head Shape

Vary awake positions and carrying sides, and offer supervised tummy time. Discuss any persistent head-turn preference or flattening with your pediatrician.

🧸 Toy Rotation & Safety

Rotating a small selection eases tidying, though there is no magic number of toys. Strictly avoid button batteries, loose magnets, sharp edges, and choking hazards.

🩺

When to Consult a Pediatrician or Physiotherapist

Do not wait for a milestone chart if you feel concerned. Contact your Czech pediatrician or a pediatric physiotherapist if you notice:

  • • Persistent asymmetry in movement or posture
  • • Unusual muscle stiffness or floppiness
  • • Signs of pain during handling or movement
  • • Loss of any previously acquired skill
  • • Persistent one-sided head-turn preference
  • • Hearing, vision, speech, or social-communication worries
The Goal: A healthy, confident child who enjoys moving and learning—supported by responsive adults, multilingual reading/talking, and safe daily play.
